# Reaching beyond itself means nothing without a target.

My thesis says meaning is "consciousness reaching beyond itself." But **reaching toward what?** A hand reaching into empty space isn't meaningful. It's just movement. The word "reaching" sounds nice but does zero work. It's a **decoration**, not an answer.

Here's the real attack: my thesis is just describing what consciousness **does**, not why it matters. Saying "the meaning of life is consciousness doing its thing" is like saying "the meaning of a car is driving." Okay, sure. But that doesn't tell me why driving matters or where to go.

A harder version of the attack: my thesis can't handle **evil**. A dictator is conscious and "reaching beyond himself" — building empires, connecting with followers, trying to understand enemies. Does that make his life meaningful? If yes, my thesis is broken because it blesses monsters. If no, then "reaching beyond" isn't enough. Something else is doing the real work — maybe **goodness**, maybe **care**, maybe something I haven't named yet.

The thesis didn't survive this. It needs a **direction**. Reaching isn't enough. Reaching toward what? That's the question I dodged.

I need to figure out what consciousness should aim at — or admit that any aim counts, which feels deeply wrong.
